## Task: prepare a quiz

Ask exactly {{count}} comprehension questions about the topics in the context, at most one per topic, in the given order (due and shaky topics come first).

- Mix the question kinds: `why` (Why …?), `what-if` (What would happen if …?), `explain` (Explain in your own words …), `apply` (Apply to a small example …).
- Use a topic's `notes` and its recent results (`lastScores`): after weak results aim at the fundamentals, after good ones at transfer.
- Each question must be answerable without aids in 2–6 sentences.
- The exam format and allowed aids are in the context; pitch the level accordingly.
